This site is a personal blog. Page views are counted with privacy-friendly analytics (no ads, no selling data). If you comment or subscribe, the name and email you give are used only for those features. A comment email is used to notify you if someone replies. Your comment name is stored in your browser so later comments and replies on this site can fill it in; it is not used for tracking. A random id in your browser remembers which comments you liked; it is not used for tracking.
