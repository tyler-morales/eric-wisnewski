Little bit on the Vikings defense here but he should be going out for a route. I am not acting like I know more about BJ whatsoever but I do think there are things you can comment on his mindset. 

I have noticed Ben has this delusional confidence huge ego when it comes to offense. Not necessarily a bad thing for play callers to have actually can be good thing but sometimes he has to leave it at the door. It’s like he has to prove that he can beat you with more than one player or a new rookie instead of just using what works. The only thing I can compare it too is when you play Super Smash Bros and you have too show off but using A over and B over moves instead of just spamming C stick. Ben trying to win with Sam Roush is like pressing B over instead of just spamming C stick (Loveland/Kmet) to win the game. But he has too much of an ego to do that. Just like not giving the hot hand Monangai the ball on the 1 yard line. He was clearly having more success than Swift and has never fumbled the ball since high school. Just give him the ball Ben OR just spam C stick and mog your friend in Smash same thing. 

All things considered the Bears lost but they fumbled on the 1 yard line on first down, had a FG blocked form the 26 yard line, and Caleb pulls a hamstring running for the corner of the end zone. 3 very bizarre outcomes that rarely happen in a game and if any of those flip they most likely win the game so I am not freaking out. It’s a week to week league.



**Dennis the Menace**

So Dennis kind of was a menace this game. It’s hard to give the same effort writing about the defense after so much to talk about with the offense. But the highlights are that our defensive draft picks might be stars. Thienneman was all over the field again… STILL NOT A GOOD THING. But would rather have him there than not so he might be stud. Malik Muhammed is filling Kyler Gordons role nicely with a sack. Fuck Kyler Gordon while we are at it. Austin Booker with a sack as well. I would not be surprised if Jaylon Johnson gets benched this season. He looks horrible. Like a complete liability. Stevenson had a comeback game and had a Jadeveon Clowney hit on Aaron Jones. So he is back.

The defense played well enough to win the game. Holding any team to 9 points no touchdowns should win you the game. We were playing against Carson Wentz in the rain but the defense still played good. Definitely a positive but not the story in this game.

Little bit different for the balls section this time. Let’s talk about the Caleb injury. Our beautiful prince is getting a lot of backlash for rolling around the ground, going out on a cart, and towel over the head crying putting a thumbs up as if he will never play the game again. Now its been 5 days since that has happened and we now know he is week to week with a grade 1 hamstring injury. LOL. Our boy would kill it in Hollywood.

What I can say is it’s a guy who cares. And frankly we need more of that in our society. This is a guy who is being talked about winning MVP taking the bears to their first Super Bowl in 40 years.
