So surely he knows about the hot hand fallacy.



The hot hand fallacy is believing someone who’s made several shots in a row is more likely to make the next one, when each shot is actually close to the independent of the last. For all the dums dums out there the stats would mean he would play like his normal QB3 self next game. SOOO my prediction will be that we are going to be learning Tyson Bagent Sunday morning. Secret Agent Agent. Ben said it himself there was a reason he was QB2 on the depth chart at the beginning of the season. Everything he has been saying during the week is pure gamesmanship against his old buddy Aaron Glenn. ALSO we know Ben Johnson likes to prove himself and prove others wrong. In his head he is saying you guys thought Keenum looked good? Imagine how much better Bagent is going to look. You liked 250 yards passing? How about 350 yards and 3 tugs. That is the type of guy he is. 



So we are going to see Bagent Sunday. I think we win. Jets are a much better team this year. Garrett Wilson might eat us alive. But clear day in Chicago, our defense gains momentum from last week, minimal mistakes on special teams and offensive penalties. I don’t see us losing but doesn’t mean it won’t be close.
