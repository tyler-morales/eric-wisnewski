Day 2 and 4: Mile 179

![](/images/uploads/IMG_1179.jpeg)

Fueled up from our feast in fleury we get into the flow of riding. 

Next is Avranches. The city sits atop a rocky hill surrounded by stunning medieval ramparts. Energized we climb the ramparts. We try to offload some gear to the post office to send to Chicago then in French we can’t understand they say something about the US president and that they won’t take our goods Arthur at the tourist office calls it scandalous when we ask him for help.

Filled with medieval buildings, cafes and squares Avranches is the most impressive town so far.
