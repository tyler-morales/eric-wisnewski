Sep 1: Mile 600

The ride is over. We made it to Bordeaux.

![](/images/uploads/IMG_1629.jpeg)

What a ride! A lot went our way especially after our slow start. Large wild fires and a full week of rain were predicted but we only had one day of bad weather in 13 days riding. Wow! Someone must be looking out for us. 

![](/images/uploads/IMG_1630.jpeg)

*Bordeaux as we approach the finish*

![](/images/uploads/IMG_1631.jpeg)

*The Bordeux reflecting pool as someone skimboards*

The final ride we were welcomed by clear sunny sky's and a tail wind. The trip went without a hitch. In the final stretch we reflected back to an encounter we had Day One with a mysterious Frenchman. We were sitting down for coffee: **two bikers on a bike trip with no bikes. Something had clearly gone wrong.** We sat dead eyed watching vacationers pass by when we were greeted the Frenchman at a neighboring table. After exchanging pleasantries we explained our plight. *We were biking with no bikes.* He listened, then paused, taking a drag of his cigaret, he proclaimed in his thick french accent **“You don’t know why now, but your bike was lost for a reason. You will figure out soon enough”**. He said it so sincerely it was unnerving. At the time we just thought he was super cool, but after 13 days of dodging storms and disaster his words seem more like a premonition. We were constantly avoiding rain and storm by minutes, seeing them just pass by on the horizon. It was so much so that it became eerie. We would sit down for coffee it would start raining and by the time we were done it was sunny. Things could not have gone more our way.

![](/images/uploads/IMG_1580.jpeg)

*Chateau Lagrange, we told them our village and they hosted us for a tasting. Lagrange like my Dad says it*

![](/images/uploads/7b700824-4ec5-4b4c-a6ab-5736b5b050eb.jpeg)

*TMO*

The ride was smooth and by the final days our pace had increased to 14mph up from 11.5mph. I feel like we could do another 400 even faster. Although we just finished,  waking up and not biking I feel a strange sense of guilt. After getting accustomed to strenuous workouts and a daily goal, whats next? Now our dismembered bikes are just stuffed into our rental. It feels like we are hiding evidence. 

![](/images/uploads/IMG_1633.jpeg)

*Hiding the evidence before car camping in Spain.*

Now we are off to Bilbao for the weekend, Germany, then Italy.

I really appreciate everyone for tuning in. Thats it for now.
