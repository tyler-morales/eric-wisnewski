Bayeux, NOR | We made it to our first stop for our bike journey, we just don’t have any bikes. After waiting an hour outside baggage the thought creeped in that my bike may not have made it. I got the help of 25yr AA vet Abel (photo’d above) and he confirmed my bike is still in Chicago. T-Mo’s jimmy rigged setup broke an hour before leaving to the airport to O’hare, out of time and out of a bike, he headed to O’hare. 

![](/images/uploads/IMG_0945.jpeg)

*Luckily I airtagged my bike before leaving*

Bayeux is old and pleasant. Not a bad place to be stuck, but we are ready to hit the road. If tomorrow passes and my bike doesn't show, I’m buying a new setup. T-Mo just bought a new bike and picked up some gear.
