Grady’s travel posts can be filtered by country. On **Grady’s Tour**, pick a country from the list or tap it on the globe. Zoom in to get closer; drag to rotate. Countries with posts show a count. That filters the list and you can share the link.

On a travel post, the country sits in the byline as a chip. On list cards it sits on the left of the photo. One country opens that filtered list. More than one country opens a dropdown so you can pick.

A post that is not in a country yet (packing, getting ready) stays on the full list.
