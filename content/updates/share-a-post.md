Tap Share next to the date at the top of a post, or again after the last photo, if you want to send it to someone. On a phone it brings up the usual list of apps. On a computer you can copy the link or send it by email, text, WhatsApp, Facebook, or X.

The same button is on Grady’s Tour and Tad’s posts too.
