When you reach the end of an article, Back to top is right there under the last photo, next to Share. It takes you to the name at the top of the page.

It is only on posts, not on the home page or the map. It does not float in the corner while you read.

If you prefer the keyboard, Tab to it and press Enter, same as any other link.
