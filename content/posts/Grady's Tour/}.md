After being out on the water so long i’ll have to slowly learn how to live on dry land again. No more rocking to sleep, views of the open sky or surviving the lakes many summer squalls. Its time to pack up and get ready for France. Thank you to all of the Firecracker crew for hosting me.

![](/images/uploads/IMG_0578-1.jpeg)

DIY bike case. I wanted to do it on the cheap so went with duct tape and corrugated plastic. All bags were bought on facebook marketplace, used bike stores and goodwill. Hopefully the carabiners and zip ties hold. The whole setup came out to $50.

The route. Why France? They seem biker friendly place for beginner bike tourers and wanted to see the DDay beaches. 

![](/images/uploads/att.XduRjy3OiT8-Uyqg-Lo8ebh0El0pBvVvWaeKLBvm-nU.jpeg)

Much of inspiration came from my friend Eichii who I met in New Orleans during his USA cross country bike ride at 80 years old! In crocs! I bought a pair and will try to follow in his croc-steps. His fortitude will be a source of inspiration I will draw on during many hard climbs. Another was my Uber driver in Florida Edwin who told me about his biking trip from Spain to Italy. It sounded amazing and told me his bike goes everywhere with him and I must do the same. @vamonos_alla
