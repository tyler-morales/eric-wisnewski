After being out on the water so long i’ll have to slowly learn how to live on dry land again. No more rocking to sleep, views of the open sky or surviving the lakes many summer squalls. Its time to pack up and get ready for France. Thank you to all of the Firecracker crew for hosting me.

![](/images/uploads/IMG_0578-1.jpeg)

DIY bike case. I wanted to do it on the cheap so went with duct tape and corrugated plastic. All bags were bought on facebook marketplace, used bike stores and goodwill. Hopefully the carabiners and zip ties hold. The whole setup came out to $50.
