This is my first blog I have ever written. I love the bears. My favorites things are watching, listening, and talking about the bears. So when my friend Eric Wisnewski started a blog and I figured why don’t I try writing about the bears too. 

The first one I am going to write is a thought I have had about Ryan Poles a couple times. IS he better at acquiring talent that have already been in the NFL rather than players coming out of college in the draft. Why? I really have no idea other than I feel like the current roster is mainly built of acquired players rather than players he drafted. So I am going to go through each starting player on the roster and where they came from.



**OFFENSE**  
Caleb Williams — QB — Draft, 2024 Round 1 Pick 1 

- Might be the best pick in bears history. Extremely strong QB draft in 2024 between Williams, Maye, & JD4 (don’t want his mom coming after me). **Career totals (2024–25):** 681/1,130 passing, 7,483 yards, 47 TD, 13 INT, 89.0 rating — his 1.2% career INT rate is the lowest in NFL history among QBs with 1,000+ attempts.

  
D'Andre Swift — RB — Free Agent, 2024 (Eagles)

- The only reason I would call myself a swifty is for Deandre Swift. . 3 years, $24M puts him roughly 15- 18th cheapest RB as the 13th rated back by PFF. **Combined Bears totals (2024–25, 27 games):** 1,608 rush yards, 10 rush TD, 4.1 YPC, 66 catches, 610 rec yards, 1 rec TD

  
Rome Odunze — WR — Draft, 2024 Round 1 Pick 9

- Odunze’s production this year vs DJ Moore will be my greatest vindication ever. Proving Poles is better at evaluating NFL talent vs. college. **Career totals (2024–25):** 98 receptions, 1,395 yards, 9 TD in 29 games

  
Luther Burden III — WR — Draft, 2025 Round 2 Pick 39

- This demon will become WR1 by the end of the year. I am curious how much of this pick is Ben Johnson over Poles. **Career totals (2025):** 47 receptions, 652 yards, 2 TD in 15 games

  
Cole Kmet — TE — Draft, 2020 Round 2 Pick 43

- The other RP (Ryan Pace) made this pick not King Poles. Interested what Poles does with his contact next year. So this doesn’t count. **Career totals (2020–2025):** 288 receptions, 2,939 yards, 21 TD

  
Colston Loveland — TE — Draft, 2025 Round 1 Pick 10

- This guy is so f’ing dreamy and good at football. Possibility of being the best TE in the league and the true Bears #1 target for Caleb. But I attribute this pick to Ben Johnson as well. **Career totals (2025):** 58 receptions, 713 yards, 6 TD in 16 games

  
Braxton Jones — LT — Draft, 2022 Round 5

- The fact that we are in a LT battle with Braxton Jones, Kiran Amegadjie, and Jedrick Willis is malpractice by Poles. 2 being his draft picks. Trapilo might be able to vindicate him **Career totals:** 46 games played, 44 starts (2022–2025)

  
Joe Thuney — LG — Trade, 2025 (Chiefs, 4th-round pick)

- Seriously the best player on the bears. So good at football and extremely based. 3 years, $51M 4th paid left guard 1st in pass protection **Totals (2025):** First-Team All-Pro, 4th Pro Bowl, inaugural **NFL Protector of the Year**

  
Garrett Bradbury — C — Trade, 2026 (Patriots, 5th-round pick)

- Would rather be talking about Drew Dalman here. If Logan Jones the second round pick plays this season I might have to edit this part. I don’t know if Eric will let me do that. I will have to ask TMO. 1 year, $4.7M

  
Jonah Jackson — RG — Trade, 2025 (Rams, 6th-round pick)

- I credit this pick up pretty much all from Ben Johnson when Jackson was on the lions from 2019-2022. But an already created NFL talent vs college. 3 years, $52.5M top 10 paid guard and 12 highest guard rated in PFF **Totals (2025):** 16 games, 16 starts, 1,078 snaps

  
Darnell Wright — RT — Draft, 2023 Round 1 Pick 10

- In my eyes this is the most clear pick made only by Ryan Poles and the contract made complete sense. Did set the market for right tackles but truly shows how you build through the draft especially on the offensive line to protect Caleb. 4 years,$116M **Career totals:** 49 games played, 49 starts (2023–2025)



**DEFENSE**  
Montez Sweat — DL — Trade, 2023 (Commanders, 2nd-round pick)

- A lot of people like to hate on Sweat. But all he needs to do is get to double digit sacks each season and he is worth it. He isn’t Myles Garrett or Micah Parsons. Just get over it edge rushers are hard to come [by.**Career**](http://by.Career) <strong>totals (2019–2025):</strong> 307 tackles, 57.0 sacks, 129 QB hits, 73 TFL, 15 FF, 21 PD, 1 INT TD (109 games)

  
Grady Jarrett — DL — Free Agent, 2025 (Falcons)

- I am getting the feeling this guy is just old and has no knees. Watching him limp around after plays is just painful. I think he was only picked up to be a veteran in the locker room which was needed **Career totals (2015–2025):** 535 tackles, 38.0 sacks, 77 TFL, 129 QB hits, 5 FF, 3 FR, 2x Pro Bowl (166 games)

  
Gervon Dexter Sr. — DL — Draft, 2023 Round 2 Pick 53

And a Poles draft pick **Career totals (2023–2025):** 115 tackles, 13.5 sacks, 42 QB hits, 3 PD, 4 FR (49 games, 33 starts)

Dayo Odeyingbo — DL — Free Agent, 2025 (Colts)

- Motivated Dayo was funny for a couple days. I tweeted out that he looked slow as soon as we picked him up. You can give him one more year to see what he has but needs 8 or more sacks to be a starter in this league. **Career totals (2021–2025):** 115 tackles, 17.5 sacks, 22 TFL, 5 FF, 3 FR (65 games, 23 starts)

  
T.J. Edwards — LB — Free Agent, 2023 (Eagles)

- Wisconsin Badger middle linebacker what more can you say. Middle linebackers are strange because it is difficult to make a major impact but you can tell when you have a bad one. Edwards is a good one but not Fred Warner. Fantastic example of NFL talent **Career totals (2019–2025):** 719 tackles, 12.0 sacks, 42 TFL, 7 INT (1 TD), 28 PD, 4 FF, 6 FR (105 games, 91 starts)

  
Devin Bush — LB — Free Agent, 2026 (Browns)

- Used to be a Cleveland Brown. 10/10 signing based off that. **Career totals (2019–2025):** 500 tackles, 7.0 sacks, 33 TFL, 5 INT (2 TD), 4 FF, 6 FR (98 games, 78 starts)

  
Coby Bryant — S — Free Agent, 2026 (Seahawks)

- RIP GET WELL SOON **Career totals (2022–2025):** 227 tackles, 7 INT (1 TD), 17 PD, 7 FF, 2 sacks (58 games, 34 starts)

  
Dillon Thieneman — S — Draft, 2026 Round 1 Pick 25

- Ben Johnson said this guy works so hard he doesn’t even know what his voice sounds like. Which is cool but he also plays safety which is concerning being a pretty vocal defensive position. But might be a generational athlete. Hopefully doesn’t turn into Chris Conte (The Italian Stallion) **College (Purdue '23–'24, Oregon '25):** 306 tackles, 8 INT, 14 PD, 10.0 TFL, 2.0 sacks, 2 FF across 39 games. Ran a 4.35 at the combine.

  
Kyler Gordon — DB — Draft, 2022, Round 2 Pick 39

- Seriously is the best player on the bears defense. But just happens to be the softest human alive. I think he will play majority of the season. But I will believe when I see it.  **Career totals (2022–2025):** 214 tackles, 5 INT, 17 PD, 2.5 sacks, 2 FF, 5 FR (44 games, 35 starts)

  
Tyrique Stevenson — CB — Draft, 2023 Round 2 Pick 56

- Absolute demon. I don’t care about the failed hail mary against the commanders. You have to have swagger at DB and Stevenson has it. Will have a big year this year **Career totals (2023–2025):** 213 tackles, 7 INT (1 TD), 38 PD, 5 FF, 2 FR
