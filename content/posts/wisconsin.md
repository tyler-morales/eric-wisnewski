About a month or two ago I was sent a link from TMO and Grady showing me that I was not alone in my journey.  It was a link to another who was trying to see all of the college basketball arenas in America, and he too also has a blog.  His name is Dan Nardini and he is from Connecticut.  Dan has been doing this from what I can tell since 2016 and continues today visiting 153 unique stadiums and counting.  If you’d like to check out Dan’s blog the link is below.  

++[https://journeyto351.com/](https://journeyto351.com/)++

I reached out to Dan to introduce myself and ask if he had any advice for someone just starting off on this journey.  He gave me a lot of advice and I’d like to thank him.  Dan mentioned that he started his journey majorly in the New England area due to proximity.  Makes sense to me, I’ll do the same with the Midwest.  Bringing us to today’s blog and location, the University of Wisconsin Madison.

Wisconsin, it is right there.  You zip in and you zip out no problem.

++[https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yr3KmyvkYBU](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yr3KmyvkYBU)++

Luckily I didn’t get my ass kicked but I did have a wonderful time on my weekend trip up norf.  The game against Coastal Carolina was on Sunday, but I was getting antsy so I decided to visit my friend Erich up in Milwaukee on Saturday night.  Erich told me “You can come but I’m doing yard work and then drinking beers”.  It sounded like a perfect relaxing weekend to me, so I went to visit my old friend.  Erich showed me around his house and explained every detail wrong with it and how he’s going to fix it.  His voice made it sound like he was annoyed, but I know deep down he’s excited to fix all this up.  Thank you Erich and Maggie for letting me crash looking forward to visiting again soon.  

![](/images/uploads/Boston/IMG_4884.jpeg)

Sunday I took off to Madison, a city that I had been to three times prior.  Once for an Iowa football game (where some people definitely wanted to kick my ass) and two other times where 6-10 people crammed into a tiny three bedroom apartment.  Shoutout to the Badgerhouse and Shannon for putting up with our bullshit on those trips.  

![](/images/uploads/Boston/IMG_4890.jpeg)

Madison is as advertised.  The first couple times I went I had a hard time admitting to myself that it is as good as it is.  I was pessimistic and trying to be a contrarian, still am today, but trying everyday to be more open. It has two lakes.  All that is needed for a Midwesterner to be happy is access to a lake, and UW Madison students are spoiled with two of them.  When Midwesterners get depressed instead of going to a therapist they go to a lake.  When they just want to stare at something, they go to a lake.  When you are trying to hide something that isn’t supposed to be found, say it with me, you go to the lake.  At UW the lake is fully utilized all year long with swimming and boating in the summer to hockey and wandering out onto the ice in the winter.  It acts as a natural recreational area for the students, something that I think most college kids wish they have.

![](/images/uploads/Boston/IMG_4889.jpeg)

My cousin is a junior at UW and she gave me a tour down State Street and around the capital.  The number of bars/restaurants, shops, theaters, and music venues is overwhelming for just one street in a college town to have.  I kept telling her I understand why people end up moving here post grad, even if they didn’t go to UW.  Madison is more than just an amazing college town, it is truly a full blown city.  

Ending the tour at the student union which already might be the best union I will see on this journey.  The building is massive and holds what most you might come to expect in most unions with offices, common study areas, and a cafe.  However there is also a creamery and a fully functioning bar inside.  If one bar wasn’t enough there is a giant patio area overlooking Lake Mendota also with a bar and brat stand.  I remember a time when we spent a few hours sitting on the patio area in July just drinking some beers, sharing some laughs, and just staring at the lake.  A Midwest special. The campus lives up to the hype.  It truly is one of the best, if not the best, college towns in all of America.  A lot to explore and see so if you do plan on visiting Madison it is probably best to do it over 2-3 days.

![](/images/uploads/Boston/IMG_4888.jpeg)

The one thing about Madison was I couldn’t tell where the campus started and where it began.  The city and the school mix really well together which was a nice touch.  That being said, technically I believe that tonight's venue, the Kohl Center, is on campus. What an arena!  Holding almost 17,000 people this is easily the largest college oriented stadium I have been to.  A relatively modern arena, the Kohl Center hosts a number of sports for Wisconsin including basketball, volleyball, and ice hockey.  Walking around the concourse they have framed jersey’s of every NHL player that has played at Wisconsin.  The number of jersey’s circle pretty much the entire arena, a subtle flex.   

The place feels like a professional arena with its high ceiling concourse and massive capacity.  About 14,000 people were in attendance, allegedly, most of which were older couples and families with young children.  I will say every single person in there was wearing red or some sort of Wisconsin attire.  I looked like an idiot wearing a gray jacket and really stuck out.  At halftime we were treated with a game of basketball connect four (boring) followed by the singing of Varsity.  Varsity, is like their school song?  Kind of a boring slow song that got the older crowd really excited, but the students they showed on screen sang it with hesitation.  Nothing more awkward when being shown on screen singing a song not knowing the words.  

![](/images/uploads/Boston/IMG_4897.jpeg)

The band was large and loud, with three rotating drummers, but they didn’t get a lot of opportunities to play as most of the music came from the PA system.  I didn't like that.  If you have the band, use the band, not that hard.

Bucky is probably a top 10 mascot. I don’t know, he looks pretty awesome, kind of adorable as well.  Got some broad ass shoulders and I saw him take a photo with a child wearing a kid version of the mascot outfit, made me chuckle.  

![](/images/uploads/Boston/IMG_4895.jpeg)

The fan base for this game was a little more timid since it was against an out of conference low rated opponent, or maybe it was also because Coastal was kind of kicking Wisconsin’s ass for a bit.  From experience though I do know how amazing their fanbase can be, and there were times when the arena was rocking.  Especially as Wisconsin senior Owen Foxwell scored 33 points that night.  Wisconsin sports fans are an excellent example of what fans should be, which pains me to say as a Chicago and Iowa fan, but I will give them their flowers.
